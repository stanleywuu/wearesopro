Stanley

Writes the recaps. Chooses violence.

Stanley started playing hockey at 18 to be cool like Crosby. He still skates like a crab walking on ice. What he could do was write: a recap after every Saturday game, then video clips, then, somehow, a whole book.

On the ice he is the one parked in front of the net, chirping the goalie. Off it, he is the one texting back “Dang it Dale, it’s 11 PM” when somebody wants the recap.

Quick facts

  • Leads his league in getting off the ice just before his team scores.
  • Won his Wednesday league three years running, until Tommy joined it.
  • Will die on the hill that he gets to the rink before Tommy.
  • Signature line: “We’re basically pro now.”
