Dale

Gives the speeches. Brings the people.

A lot of this group exists because of Dale. Back when nobody could find a goalie, he bought a shooting tutor off Kijiji. When someone in the chat demanded games every week, Dale snapped: “Since you are the one wanting to play every week, why don’t you organize something?” Stanley’s first ever message to him was “Thanks, Dale.”

He avoids drills, early Saturdays and, most weeks, parked cars. He never misses a chance for a big speech about how far everyone has come. He is retired now, and drives a school bus.

Quick facts

  • Biggest connector: he turned Tommy into a people person.
  • Counted how many times every character is mentioned in the finished book.
  • Expects the recap on time, even at 11 PM.
  • Signature line: “You don’t need to sharpen your skates.”
