Tommy The Goalie

Big and serious on the outside, goofy on the inside.

Tommy is the goalie everyone counts on to show up, every single week. He can stand on his head for a whole game, then watch a harmless dump-in from centre ice roll past him in the final. His teams have a losing record in championship games, and the group has started calling it Tommy’s curse.

Dale turned Tommy, a self-described people hater, into the connector of the group. When the book was finished he stayed up until 4 AM reading it, with a game the next day.

Quick facts

  • Most groin saves of any Early Birds goalie: five, and counting.
  • Styles up his stick for every new team he joins.
  • Once started skating to Stanley’s bench mid-game. Stephanie sent him back.
  • Signature line: “I hate people.” He introduced me to half my friends.
