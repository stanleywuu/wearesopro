Ricky

Loudest voice on the ice. No explanation needed.

You hear Ricky before you see him. He yells when he scores, when he misses, and when he is just having fun, which is most of the time. It took Stanley 30 minutes to find a clip of Ricky laughing like a maniac, and now that laugh is in every highlight video.

He has missed a lot of open nets, once three in ten seconds. Then he scored a hat trick and brought his kids to the championship game. “They are not coming next time,” he said after. They came next time, and banged on the glass.

Quick facts

  • Booked a cruise on championship day. A heat wave postponed the game.
  • Plays defence, where he had three points all season.
  • Voted loudest voice. No explanation needed.
  • Signature line: “Woohoo! This is so much fun!”
