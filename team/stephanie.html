Stephanie

Runs the drills. Owns the whistle.

Stephanie turned a group of adults who could barely skate into the Early Birds, and she picked the name too. She plans the Saturday drills, makes the teams on Friday night, and does most of the organizing on and off the ice.

Her drill sheets say things like “This continues until everyone drops to the floor :)”. Nobody wants to upset Stephanie. She is tough, but she is not evil: some mornings it is only one lap.

Quick facts

  • Voted the most appreciated Early Bird.
  • Her team lost one game all season: the championship final. Nobody is allowed to mention it.
  • Showed up one morning with a whistle. Things got worse from there.
  • Signature line: “I’m lowkey angry at some people.”
