This book was the result of misguided positive reinforcement.

I'm so glad I went in blind, thinking I could do this. I mean who do I think I am?

The book itself started off with my wanting to include all my friends who coudldn't make it to our weekly pickup hockey sessions and missed out on all the fun moments that happened. One day, I just said to myself: "This would've been such a great premise for an anime." Sports, but not about a special talent, not about teenagers. It's about older people with jobs, just trying to enjoy themselves.

Then I decided to ask AI what it took

Making an anime would would take way too much effort. Then I paid OpenAI for a pro subscription to analyze my content. It told me this could be so good. And I believed it...

It lied

I realized how bad it actually was when I started editing: "This is what you said was good? Are you kidding?"

But I'm glad I bought into positivity

Because it actually is really good now!
