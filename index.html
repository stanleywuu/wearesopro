From the desk

This book was the result of misguided positive reinforcement.

I went in blind, thinking I could do this. I mean, who do I think I am?

It started because I wanted to include the friends who couldn’t make it to our weekly pickup sessions and missed all the good moments. One day I said to myself: “This would’ve been such a great premise for an anime.” Sports, but not about a special talent and not about teenagers — older people with jobs, just trying to enjoy themselves.

An anime was far too much effort, so I paid OpenAI for a pro subscription to analyze my content instead. It told me this could be so good. And I believed it.

It lied.

I found out how bad it actually was once I started editing: “This is what you said was good? Are you kidding?” But I’m glad I bought into the positivity — because it really is good now.

Good enough that you might want a few different copies as gifts, which begs the question…
